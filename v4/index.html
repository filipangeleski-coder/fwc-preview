05Straight answers
What is the $20, exactly?

A refundable deposit. It holds your approximate spot in the queue for the first 500 pairs. It is not a purchase, and you are under no obligation to ever buy a boot.

How do I get it back?

Email us or reply to any email we have sent you. No questions. It goes back to the card you paid with within 5 business days.

When do they ship?

We won't give you a date we can't hold. The first samples are in our hands. The next thing to lock in is how many pairs we make and in what sizes. You'll hear before anyone else, and we'd rather be late than ship a boot that falls apart.

What if you never make them?

Every deposit comes back in full, automatically. You don't have to ask.

Where are they made?

Designed in Australia, made overseas, built to AS/NZS 2210.3. We're not going to dress that up.

Are they waterproof?

No. Water resistant. A waterproof boot is a different boot and we'd rather tell you that now than at the first wet day.

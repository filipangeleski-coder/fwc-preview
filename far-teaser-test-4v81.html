Presale teaser: first motion test

Made in code from our own boot photos. No AI-generated boot, no stock footage. A test, not a post. Sound's off on purpose: music goes on in CapCut.

The feed version (4:5)

What to judge

  • Does it look like a real brand made it, or like an AI demo? Your eye decides this one, not a checklist.
  • The pace. 19.5 seconds, a hard cut every one to two seconds.
  • "You told her they're fine. / She's seen your feet." It's the most personal beat. Keep it, or swap in a different line from the website.
  • Would you post it the week the presale opens?

How it was made

  • Every boot in it is a real photo: Don's studio shoot and our own close-ups. The studio boots were cut off their white background onto black. The boot pixels themselves are untouched.
  • Every line is from the website copy that already passed our voice checks. The ending uses "search FAR Workwear", word for word.
  • Brand kit rules, built into the code: black background, white text only, text cuts in and out with no animation, no boxes or shapes over the photos. The only movement is the camera on the photos.
  • The logo is the real FAR wordmark in white on black, the same way the website header shows it.
  • Rendering one version takes about 16 seconds on the laptop. Changing a line, a photo or the timing and re-rendering takes minutes, not a re-edit.

Checked before you saw it

  • A brand review caught a Monster can in the corner of the first photo and a computer screen behind the badge close-up. Both are cropped out now.
  • "Being made now" is true: the 500 are in production, with a 28 Nov finish in Don's writing.
  • No price, no date, no water or comfort claims.
If this direction works, the next test swaps in real footage of the boot on a foot for the opening shot. The brand kit wants something happening in frame one, and these are still photos.

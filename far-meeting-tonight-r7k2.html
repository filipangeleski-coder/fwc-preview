What actually makes a bloke pay up front

The numbered pair is out. Nothing anywhere says a tradie gets his wallet out because he is number 47, that mechanic comes from sneaker culture. It stays as a nice touch on the note in the box, not as the reason.

The answer was already written and we have never used it
There is a FAR promise sitting in our own files that has never appeared on the website: 18 months on manufacturing faults, a 30 day on-the-job test where if they are wrong on site you refund or replace, 12 months on water resistance, and 40 percent off a replacement pair between months 6 and 12. That is the thing that answers a bloke handing 219 dollars to a brand he has never heard of. It is a guarantee, not a gimmick, and it costs nothing unless we are wrong about the boot. It needs a lawyer to read it before it goes live, which is the same 500 dollar job we have been putting off since July.

The lifetime boots draw. Checked properly today, and the idea survives with three changes.

  • Do not call it lifetime. Bound it. Two pairs a year for three years, to two winners, keeps the prize value under every state threshold in the country, which means no permit anywhere. Call it lifetime and it crosses the ACT limit inside two years and needs approval before we can even advertise it.
  • There has to be a free way to enter. Buyers-only is not a promotion, it is an unlawful lottery. A free entry path with the same odds, sitting next to the paid one, is not optional.
  • Draw it after the boots land, not at presale open. We already made this exact call once before on a different competition: the prize cannot be boots until boots exist.

Wilder: the other new boot brand

Fil found them. Same factory as ours, launching in October. Everything on this page was checked on 26 September, not guessed. Read to the bottom: two of the calls are yours.

Who they are

  • A Melbourne company, Made Wilder Pty Ltd, registered in June 2025. They tried five names before landing on Wilder.
  • Their boot is built at the same factory as ours. Don told Fil in July. Don also said they were buying 12,000 pairs. Nobody has been able to check that, and their Instagram has a business-to-business tab, so a fleet contract is the likely story if it is true.
  • Instagram: 105 followers and four posts since 9 September. Their best post got 8 likes and no comments. We have 3,479 followers and our last big reel did 357,000 views.
  • Their site is a locked page with a countdown. Waitlist opens 8 October. A holiday giveaway opens 9 October, prize list and terms not published yet. Launch around 26 October. Waitlist gets 25% off the launch price.
  • Their tagline: "Premium Aussie work boots for the discerningly filthy". Nothing about the boot itself except waterproof zips and lace hooks. No price, no safety rating, no materials, no country of origin shown.
  • The best thing they do: two emails within hours of signing up, the second a long letter from the founder.

What they are not

A threat on social. One of our reels has more shares than they have followers.

A reason to panic about being second. The research on new markets says about half of the first movers fail and the early followers take the share. BRUNT, the American brand we watch, launched six years after the first online boot brands and leads them all now. BRUNT did run giveaways, with forty-four million US dollars of investor money and paid ads behind them. That is the point: a giveaway is a tool for a brand with cash to burn.

A copy of our enemy posts that works. Their post copies the shape of ours, naming what is wrong with the boot industry without naming a brand, but it points the finger at the tradie ("you're being farmed"). Ours point at the industry. Theirs got 8 likes.

What they are: two real things

1. Their boot may be close to ours underneath

Ours is a certified factory model with our collar and lining. If theirs is the same base boot, then what makes FAR different on 26 October is the story, the fit work in millimetres, the money-back on worn boots, and the proof we film. That was true before Wilder existed. Wilder puts a date on it.

2. Don talks to both of us

He told us about them, so he tells them about us. The collar foam, the water-repellent leather, the size table, the magnetic box: none of that is protected at the factory. Only our artwork and our name are.

Decided

  • No launch giveaway. The prize cannot be boots we do not have yet. Our own email list has sent three campaigns for three clicks and zero orders, so a bigger list is not the problem. Giveaway sign-ups want a holiday, not boots.
  • No chasing tool brands for free gear. It costs hours we do not have, gets a no or a token, and puts their logo on our launch.
  • The FAR version of a giveaway is the dead-boot amnesty in December, once boots exist: bring in your cooked pair, best story wins a pair. No permit needed for a skill contest, and every dead boot is content.
  • The box is the giveaway for the first 150. The site already says there is more in it. Add nothing that is not ours.
  • We never name Wilder anywhere public, and we never mention them to Don.

What Wilder shows up at our end

  • Someone who signs up with us today gets one email, then nothing. That email still says first 100 and still promises free boots for referrals, which we decided against on 8 September. The proper email sequence is written but not switched on.
  • Our Instagram bio still says four mates and claims Australia's comfiest boot.
  • TIACS: we agreed to partner with them on 8 September. The email has been sitting in drafts since 22 September.
  • When the pre-sale opens is still not voted. Fil says when production finishes, Lionel says when boots are in hand, Grant has not voted.

The short version
They are first to open a list. We are first to have shown a boot destroyed on camera, first to put three blokes on the tools behind it, and the only ones with an audience. Their date is a useful clock for our own loose ends, not a reason to change the plan.

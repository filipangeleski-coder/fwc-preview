The Burnout v8 (fresh crack)

Download v8

How it was made: three fresh reviewers pulled apart every version and Fil's notes, three independent edit ideas were written, a judge picked one, the critics checked the lines, then it was built and a cold "never seen it before" viewer tore into it.

  • No ball, no borrowed clip. It opens on our own boots being driven over: the tyre rolls on, the boot gets kicked up and spat out.
  • Fil's voice from the first frame: "That's a thousand bucks down the drain" runs over the crush instead of after a 2.5 second wait.
  • The hook line is YOUR LAST PAIR. It's about the viewer's own boots, not a riddle. FAR's best posts open on something the viewer recognises, and the critics ranked this first of four.
  • Kept: the pull-out, the invoice landing on "name", the build order popping on the first word on the grass.

The honest part

  • It's 17.8 seconds. That's shorter than the 16.8 s version Fil called rushed. The cold viewer said they'd stop for the crush but would leave around the cut to Fil walking in, about 2 seconds in.
  • The cold viewer also said the pile of boots behind the invoice looks smeared, which is the brand blurs on the dead boots, and that the paper cards (invoice, build order, the HEAD OFFICE card) read a bit like an ad.
  • The funniest shot, the dive on the grass, still sits at 13 seconds, after most people have gone.

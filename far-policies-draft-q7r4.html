1. A phone number

The law says a written warranty must show a phone number (Competition and Consumer Regulations reg 90). Email alone doesn't count. It also goes on the Contact page Shopify marks Required.

My rec
Get a second number you're happy to have public (a cheap second SIM) before money opens. Until then, the 6-month warranty stays off the site. The 30-day return can go up without it.

2. Warranty clock

6 months from purchase, or from delivery?

My rec
From delivery. A presale buyer pays months before the boots arrive. Counting from purchase would eat half the warranty before they've worn them.

3. Warranty fallback

If we can't replace a faulty pair in their size.

My rec
Refund them.

4. Refund speed

How fast refunds go back.

My rec
Within 5 business days of the boots reaching us.

Order number only. No box, no receipt. Matches 'no questions asked'.

The homepage FAQ says December 2026 to early January 2027. By sea the boots land in the first week of January, so December only works if the first pairs fly, and that vote is reopened. Under consumer law a date you give needs reasonable grounds.

Your rights under Australian Consumer Law

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

In plain terms:

  • Major failure: you choose a refund or a replacement. A replacement is owed if the goods are reasonably available. If not, you get a refund.
  • Minor failure: we fix it free. We choose to repair, replace or refund, within a reasonable time.
  • If we do not fix a minor failure in a reasonable time, you can choose a refund or a replacement, or get it fixed elsewhere and we pay the reasonable cost.
  • Refunds go back the way you paid, with no deduction for wear.
  • These rights are not cut off at 30 days or 6 months. They last for a reasonable time.
  • You do not need to use FAR's 30-day return or the warranty to use your legal rights.

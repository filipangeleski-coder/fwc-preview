Why a bag, not a box

  • A printed bag costs cents and adds no volume. Air freight is priced by volume, and 150 pairs fly.
  • The slides sit inside our own box, so the bag only has to look right when the box opens.
  • Size on the bag shows EU and AU, the same pairing as the boot labels.

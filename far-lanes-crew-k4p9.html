How FAR content works

For the crew. Why we post what we post, when, and who it's for. One page. Read it once.

Why we post at all

Nobody buys boots from a brand they've never seen. Content does three jobs, in order:

1. ReachPull in strangers. Comedy does this. Our comedy has hit 280 thousand views on one post and 4.2 million on the biggest ever.
2. TrustMake them believe the boot. Proof, the build story, the craft. These posts do 2 to 15 thousand views but they're the ones that turn a viewer into a buyer.
3. SellThe bio link, pinned comments, the email list. Soon: deposits and launch. We never sell inside a comedy video. Ever.

We're sitting at about 3,300 followers with millions of cumulative views. That gap is the whole game plan: reach is working, so the next phase is trust and sell. Your comedy keeps the front door open. The trust posts are what the launch depends on.

Who we're talking to

For reach: anyone with a job. The nurse, the warehouse worker, the office worker. If a joke needs trade knowledge to land, it caps out small. If it's a feeling anyone who's ever had a boss knows, it travels. Quick test before filming a bit: would a nurse laugh at this?

For trust and sell: the tradie. 28 to 45, on his feet 10 hours, feet cooked, been told to harden up his whole career. He buys off a mate's word, never off an ad. That's why we never sound like an ad.

The lanes

Comedy you own this

What: skits, characters, site life. The Micromanager, the Old Fella, the New Guy. The sketch IS the point.

Job: reach. Strangers find FAR because they got sent a video by a mate.

Posts: Tuesday and Friday, 7pm. Never two comedy days back to back.

  • No selling. No "link in bio". No mentioning FAR in the video. The boot can be in it, never pitched.
  • Never tell people to share, tag, save, or follow. If it's funny they send it themselves. If we ask, they don't.
  • The funny is in the reaction, not the words. Cut on the reaction.
  • It has to work with the sound OFF for the first second. That's what stops the thumb.
  • Swearing is fine if it's natural. Never in the on-screen text.

Proof evidence

What: tests, teardowns, what's inside the boot, wear trials. Real numbers on camera.

Job: trust. The tradie deciding whether we're for real.

  • Never fake anything. If we show it, we did it.
  • Never name another brand. Describe the class ("the $250 boot"), let him fill in the name.
  • If we claim it, we've tested it. No claim without proof.

The craft obsession

What: the boot itself. The guts, the samples we rejected, why we built it this way.

Job: trust, the deep kind. Quiet expert voice. No hype words, the detail does the talking.

Posts: rare on purpose. One or two a month so it stays special.

The industry enemy

What: saying what every tradie already knows. Six month warranties. Insoles you bin on day one. "Premium" prices on boots that die by winter.

Job: he watches it and thinks "see, I was right". That's trust too.

  • Punch at the system, never at a worker, never at a named brand.
  • Max one a week, usually less. Too much and we're just a whinge account.

The build founder

What: building FAR in public. The deposits, the samples, the factory fights, the stock landing. Any of the three founders on camera, talking straight.

Job: the strongest converter per view we have. People back people.

  • Real numbers, real decisions. Never vague feelings.
  • Hi-vis on when a founder talks to camera. Tested: without it the video dies.

The three gates before anything posts

  • 1. Would a tradie actually run into this on site? If it wouldn't happen, it doesn't post.
  • 2. Does the first second stop the thumb with no sound? Action or a reaction, never a still.
  • 3. Has it happened to HIM? He has to feel it, not just watch it. This one's non-negotiable.

What you can film without waiting on anyone

  • Comedy bits with the crew. You're already doing it. Keep going.
  • Wear trial check-ins: 20 seconds at knock-off, "week three in the V2s, here's the honest read". Feet, comfort, what surprised you. These become the proof bank.
  • Banter and site moments on your phone. Raw is better than polished. Send everything to the Drive folder, don't sit on it.
  • Before filming: no other company's logo on caps, shirts or hi-vis in frame. That includes machinery brands on caps. One logo in shot can kill a whole clip.

The quick no list

  • Never tell viewers to share, tag, save or follow.
  • Never name or show another boot brand.
  • Never say "early access". It's the Founding Crew.
  • Never say waterproof. The boot is water resistant and we're honest about it.
  • No end cards, no "thanks for watching", no outros. The video just ends.
  • No other companies' branding on clothes in frame.

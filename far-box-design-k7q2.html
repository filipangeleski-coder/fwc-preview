What you are looking at

These are not mock-ups. The box is built in 3D at its real size, 360 by 320 by 130, from the exact print files Don's printer will get. The matte black and the gloss are set up the way the real finishes behave, so the gloss only shines when a light bounces off it into your eye, same as in your hand.

A render is still not a box. The only real test is a photo of one finished box from Don before the 500 get made, and we are asking him for it.

Three ways to print the lid

White: the big FAR is bare card, the loudest. Charcoal: the FAR is printed dark grey with gloss over it. Premium: the FAR is black gloss on matte black, no ink, like the inspiration boxes. On charcoal and premium a small white FAR sits on the front flap so the brand always shows from the front.

Kitchen table, ceiling light

This is the one that matters most. It is where most unboxing videos get filmed, ours and every customer's. White reads from across the room. Charcoal reads. Premium goes quiet: the FAR is there as darker shiny letters if you look for it.

When the light catches it

Photo-studio light, placed so the gloss flashes. All three look sharp. This is the look in the inspiration pictures, and it only happens at the right angle.

Outdoors, grey sky

On a slab or a ute tray the gloss mirrors the bright sky, so even premium reads clearly outside.

Our read: charcoal
It keeps the premium feel in the hand and still puts the FAR in every unboxing shot, in any light. The case against: it is the middle option, not as loud as white and not as pure as premium. White is what Fil first asked for ("Loud FAR on the front") and costs nothing extra on the files as built.

Open the lid

The tray is shown empty. The boots, socks and bag go in here. The photo inside the lid is a frame grab from the garage shoot, standing in until we take the real one.

The story inside the lid

Under the photo of the three of us, two short lines, then WE REFUSE TO ACCEPT IT. Here are all three, zoomed so you can read them the way he will.

1. The deal

You give the job your back, your weekends and your knees.
Your feet shouldn't be part of the deal.
WE REFUSE TO ACCEPT IT.

About him, not us. Says feet without selling the boot. The quietest of the three, and "part of the deal" is a phrase people have heard.

2. The slide

Sore by smoko. Wrecked by knock-off. Back in them at five.
Somewhere along the way you accepted it. So did we.
WE REFUSE TO ACCEPT IT.

The best set-up for the last line, and the only one with the three of us in it. Risk: "wrecked by knock-off" can read as knock-off boots, not knock-off time.

3. The best part

Best part of your day? Boots off at the ute.
It shouldn't be.
WE REFUSE TO ACCEPT IT.

Shortest and punchiest. Opens with a question, which is the most common trick in ads, and leans on the day being bad.

Our read: 1, the deal
Clearest in a second, about him, and safe to print 500 times. If you want the one with the most bite, it is 2.

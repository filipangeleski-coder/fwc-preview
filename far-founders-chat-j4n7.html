2. Sizing the 500

Where it stands. We sell AU 6 to 13, and AU is the same as UK for men, whole sizes only. In the factory our size becomes a Euro size 35 higher, so AU 9 is Euro 44. Don's mould stops at Euro 47, which is AU 12. Euro 48, our AU 13, is only free above 1,000 pairs in total; otherwise it is USD 1,430 and a three month wait. So AU 13 is not on run one unless we pay for it and wait. The size lock has to be made in a thick work sock: the call between 46 and 47 sits on 7 millimetres. There is no per size table anywhere yet.

The sign-up list already holds 22 boot sizes from the early form (the later 102 contest sign-ups had no size field): AU 5: 1, AU 7: 1, AU 8: 2, AU 9: 4, AU 10: 6, AU 11: 2, AU 12: 5, AU 13: 1. Small, but it points the same way as the trade read: sizes 9 to 12 carry 17 of the 22, and 12 is heavier than a normal curve. The starting curve below leans that way.

Outside reference, not our data: with no sales history, footwear buyers use a middle weighted curve, something like 1:2:3:2:1 across a five size run. The UK and AU men's average is size 9, and the top four or five sizes usually carry 60 to 70 per cent of the units.

Google's emails, in plain English

"Monitor the Google Search traffic" Good news

Google started showing FAR in search results on Wed 8 Oct. The "Improve Google presence" email is just Google's welcome after we set up Search Console. Nothing to do on either.

"New reasons prevent pages from being indexed"

Sounds bad. Mostly it isn't. Each reason:

  • Blocked by robots.txt. Cart, checkout and account pages. Shopify hides these on purpose. Fine.
  • Page with redirect. The old Founding Crew page (it forwards home now), plus the www and http versions of our address. On purpose. Fine.
  • Alternative page with proper canonical tag. The same page with extra bits on the end of the address. Google keeps the main one. Fine.
  • Excluded by 'noindex'. Pages Shopify tells Google to skip, like search results. Almost certainly fine. Your screenshot below confirms it.
  • Not found (404). The one that matters. Refund, shipping and terms don't exist yet. The blog and catalogue we deleted on 9 Oct are in here too, and those are fine. Google drops them on its own.

The fix is the policy pages (section 3), not anything in Search Console. I couldn't open Search Console myself, so this matches each reason to what I found crawling the site this morning.

"Product snippets structured data issues"

The homepage tells Google about the FAR Zip but gives no price, because there's nothing to buy yet. So Google won't show the boot as a product result with a price. It doesn't push us down in search.

Leave it. The price goes in the day the paid page opens (section 5). Putting one in now would tell Google you can buy something you can't.

4. Before the private pre-sale page opens

What the Australian law check turned up. It's research for the lawyer, not legal advice, but every point here comes from the law itself or Shopify's own rules.

  • The delivery date we print is a legal promise. Only take money for a window we're sure of, with a real latest date, not "early 2027". The date we give also starts the clock on how long a buyer's bank can reverse the payment. All four
  • No "refer a mate, get credit when they buy". Australian law bans rewards that depend on someone else buying later. Personal asks are fine. A buy-and-earn scheme isn't. All four
  • The bloke who hit 10 referrals is legally owed his boots. A promised gift has to be honoured. He's on the honour list. All four
  • Shopify can hold pre-sale money. It names pre-orders as a reason to hold back payouts. Its own examples are 10% or a set amount for 120 days. If any pre-sale money is meant for the factory balance, ask Shopify support before the page opens. Fil
  • Hide the pre-sale product. Shopify lists every published product in Shop and the AI shopping tools by default. The pre-sale boot has to be set to Unlisted, or the private page isn't private. Claude
  • Payment has to be taken at checkout. Any other setting lets the card hold expire after 7 days. Fil
  • The 6-month warranty needs exact government wording on something the buyer gets, most likely a printed card in the box. It's on the lawyer's list. Fil
  • Order emails need "FAR WORKWEAR PTY LTD" and the ABN. Trading as plain "FAR Workwear" probably needs a registered business name, which is cheap. It's on the lawyer's list. Fil
  • Order emails should come from our address, not a Shopify one. That needs a domain setting (it's in the screenshots above). Fil
  • The certificate line. The page says "Certified to AS 2210.3:2019". Before money is taken, check that wording against the certificate itself. Fil
  • TikTok pixel. It's still not on the site. It needs a decision on whether it's worth it, then your TikTok login. Fil

Not doing

  • Paying an "AI search" agency, or chasing gimmicks like llms.txt files. Neither moves anything.
  • Letting any of this eat filming or editing time. Content brings people in. Search is where they come to check us out.
  • A Google Business Profile. Google doesn't allow one for an online-only seller working from home.
  • Shopify's "Crawler access" setting. It's only for paid SEO tools. Google and Bing don't need it.

Getting FAR found online

What the website needs so Google, AI tools and buyers can find it and trust it. Everything here was checked on the live site on Wed 7 Oct.

The short version

  • The homepage is in decent shape. It has a proper title, a Google description, a share image, and the business details Google reads behind the scenes.
  • The rest of the site isn't. Three policy pages are missing. The privacy page shows your home street. Google has never been told the site exists. The other pages are blank Shopify defaults.
  • None of it is big. Most of it I can do on a test copy of the site. About 30 minutes needs you, because it needs your logins.
Honest bit
Google won't sell the first 100. Your personal asks will. Two things here matter no matter what: the policy pages (needed before any money changes hands) and the privacy fix. The rest is cheap but slow, because Google takes weeks to pick up a site. That's why it's worth starting now.

1. Today or tomorrow: you

Needs your Shopify or Google login. About 30 minutes, phone or laptop.

Take your street off the privacy page Fil

The privacy page ends with "FAR Workwear, Robsons Road, Keiraville NSW 2500", and it says "please call" with no number. Anyone can read it.

Shopify > Settings > Policies > Privacy policy. Change the address to "Wollongong NSW 2500" until the PO box is open, and delete "call or". 5 minutes.

Find out what "Propel replays" is Fil

A script with that name loads on every visit to the site. It looks like an app that records visitors' screens. I can't see the app list from here, so I can't tell you what it is.

Shopify > Apps. If nobody's using it, uninstall it. It's also slowing the page down. 5 minutes.

Set up Google Search Console Fil

This is how you tell Google the site exists, and how we see what people type to find us. It has never been set up. It needs your Google login plus a one-line record on the domain. You start it, send me the code it gives you, and I tell you exactly where it goes.

search.google.com/search-console > Add property > Domain > farworkwear.com.au. 15 minutes.

Tick "prices include tax" Fil

It's off right now. Already on your list for 24 Oct, and it's a 2-minute job.

Shopify > Settings > Taxes and duties.

I'll hide the empty ones, put the contact page in FAR's look, give each page a real description, and pull the hidden pages out of the list Google reads.

  • Update our search plan. It's three months out of date.

3. Before any money changes hands

Before the private pre-sale page opens in early November.

Refund, shipping and terms pages Hard gate

All three show "page not found" today. Google won't list a shop without them. Australian consumer law also wants refund rights spelled out before you take money.

I draft them from what we've already ruled: 30 days worn from the day they arrive, and a 6-month warranty on faults. Then they go to the lawyer read due 16 Oct.

  • TikTok pixel. It's still not on the site, three months after it was planned. It needs a decision on whether it's worth it, then your TikTok login. Fil
  • Hide the private pre-sale page from Google, so it doesn't show up in search.
  • Check our emails land in the inbox, not spam. The welcome email and the pre-sale emails are the whole pre-sale channel.
  • The Google description. It says "$219 for the first 100", so it goes stale the day they sell out. A founder call on new wording.
  • The certificate line. The page and hidden data say "Certified to AS 2210.3:2019". Before money is taken, check that wording against the certificate itself.

4. At launch

When a real product page exists, December to January.

  • Price data on the boot, so Google and AI tools can show it with a price.
  • A Google Shopping listing. The Google app is already installed in Shopify, it just has nothing to list yet.
  • Review emails after delivery, and a ProductReview.com.au page.

Not doing

  • Paying an "AI search" agency, or chasing gimmicks like llms.txt files. Neither moves anything.
  • Letting any of this eat filming or editing time. Content brings people in; search is where they come to check us out.
  • A Google Business Profile. It's probably not allowed for an online seller working from home. Check before applying.

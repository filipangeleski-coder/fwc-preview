Every day, mostly unseen

What changes at launch

Right now it is busy but steady. About 113 jobs tick over week to week.

Then launch lands and two things happen at once:

  • 16 jobs squeeze into one week. Launch morning, the messages, the posting, the packing, the physical drops. All on the same days.
  • 31 new jobs start and never stop. Customers. Orders going out, sizes coming back, warranty claims, reviews. That work did not exist before and it does not go away.

That is the bit nobody plans for. Which is exactly why we are having this conversation now and not the week it hits.

What we are asking today

  1. What do you want to take? Green first. Amber if you fancy it, the check has your back.
  2. What do you not want to touch? Say so now. Nobody is being handed something they hate.
  3. What are we missing? If something on here looks dumb or backwards, say it. None of this is locked.

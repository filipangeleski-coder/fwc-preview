Stickers: pick five

Carol is making 1,000 stickers for us: 5 designs, 200 of each. They're already paid for in her order. Here are 11 options. Claude's pick of 5 is marked. Carol needs the artwork by Wed 22 Oct.

What they are
Outdoor vinyl, cut to shape, with a thin white edge like Carol's proof. Most are 8 cm wide, about the width of a bank card. They go in the boxes and get handed out. Every one carries the FAR logo, placed from our real logo file, never typed.

Good to know

  • Carol quoted on an 8 x 4 cm sticker. The boot and a few others are taller, so her price may move a little. We check with her before we confirm.
  • Hard hats: we asked for a water-based glue that's safe on the plastic. Stick it on the shell only, never over the brim edge or the hat's own labels.
  • "Farked" and "arse" are mild, but some sites are funny about words on hats. Worth knowing before you hand one to a foreman.
  • Carol also asked for our logo as a vector file. That's sorted: every logo, black and white, ready to send her.

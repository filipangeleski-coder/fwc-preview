Straight questions

The full bank for the ute-cabin videos (STDS): 90 questions. Pick one. Don't say the question on camera, start on your first real line. Leave the silences in. If it gets too much, shake your head and stop, that's a take too. Only say what you've actually lived. The one that set the bar was the alarm one: 78,670 views, because it named a moment every tradie has had and nobody says out loud.

Your body

  • When you sit down in the ute at knock-off and your lower back makes that noise, what actually goes through your head?
  • That first step out of bed in the morning, have your heels always felt like that?
  • There is a point in the afternoon where your feet go numb. You know the one. When does it happen for you?
  • Have you ever stretched your toes inside your boots and felt nothing?
  • Have you ever put your boots on and the damp from yesterday is still inside?
  • What is the first part of your body that tells you it is Monday?
  • Your hands shake after a big day on the jackhammer. You pretend they do not. How long has that been going on?
  • You know that thing where you wake up before the alarm and your body is already tense? What is that?
  • Have you ever stood up after smoko and your knees just do not work for the first few steps?
  • That ache behind your kneecaps by Wednesday. Is that new or has it always been there?

The head stuff

  • Have you ever sat in the ute for ten minutes before going in because you just needed the silence?
  • Have you ever caught yourself counting down the hours before the shift has even properly started?
  • Do you ever get home and realise you have been clenching your jaw the entire drive?
  • Is there a moment on the drive home where it actually hits you, like properly hits you, how tired you are?
  • When the alarm goes off and you do not move. Just lie there. What is happening in your head during those seconds?

What nobody asks about

  • Does anyone in your life actually know what your feet look like at the end of a week?
  • Have you ever gone to the chemist for your feet and not told anyone?
  • When is the last time you took a day off because your body was done, not sick, just done?
  • Has a physio or chiro ever told you something about your body that genuinely scared you?
  • Has anyone in your family ever said something about the way you walk?
  • Have you ever Googled something about your body at 11pm and not liked what you found?

What the job has cost you

  • Is there something you used to do easily that you have quietly stopped being able to do?
  • When you look at your dad's hands, or his back, or his knees, do you see yourself in 20 years?
  • Have you ever been out on the weekend and someone noticed you were moving differently, limping, stiff, slow, and you had to explain it?
  • You know when a young bloke starts on site and he is bouncing around like nothing hurts yet. What do you actually think when you see that?
  • Do you ever look at blokes in offices and think about what their body feels like at 5pm?
  • Is there a moment where you realised this job was going to cost your body more than you planned?
  • Before you started this trade, what did you think your body would feel like at 35? What does it actually feel like?
  • When you signed up, what did you think the hardest part would be? What turned out to be the actual hardest part?
  • What did you think a knock-off beer would feel like before you started? What does it actually feel like now?
  • Day 1 apprentice you, what did he think the boss would be like? What did the boss actually turn out to be?
  • What did you think you would do for fun on weekends when you started this trade? What do weekends actually look like?

Mornings

  • Is there a version of you that existed before you started waking up stiff?
  • The first five minutes on site, ute door closed, before the work starts. What does your body do?
  • Do you stretch before work or have you given up on that?
  • What do you tell yourself at 5:30am to actually get out of bed?

The silence

  • Is there something you have wanted to say to someone on site but did not, because you would have to admit something?
  • When was the last time you actually told someone, anyone, how your body really feels?
  • If someone handed you a piece of paper and said write down every part of you that hurts right now, how long would that list be?

The heavy ones (one per filming, max)

  • Is there a version of tired that sleep does not fix?
  • Do you ever get home and feel like the day followed you in through the door?
  • What do you do with a bad day on site, where does it actually go?
  • Have you ever been on site and felt completely switched off, not tired, just gone?
  • Is there a point in the week where your patience runs out and someone at home cops it first?
  • When you are actually honest with yourself, is this job making you a better person to be around at home?
  • Have you ever kept going on a really bad mental day just because you could not work out how to explain it to anyone?
  • Is there something you are carrying from this job that you have never actually put down?
  • Do you ever feel like the bloke on site and the bloke at home are two different people?
  • What does the job do to you by Friday that you spend the whole weekend trying to undo?
  • Have you ever pulled up to the site entrance and just had to sit there for a bit before walking in?
  • Is there a version of you that you think the job took?
  • Is there something you do to manage the heavy weeks that you have never told anyone about?

Deeper (only once you are already somewhere real)

  • Do blokes on your crew ever admit when they are in pain? What actually happens?
  • When did you stop expecting to feel good at knock-off?
  • What would you do if you physically could not do this job anymore?
  • Is there something you are proud of from this job that you will never say out loud?
  • What would you say to a young bloke who is just starting out and already feeling it?
  • What keeps you showing up?
  • How long does your body have left in this trade?
  • Have you ever driven home and genuinely not known how you made it?
  • How many times have you thought this week is not worth it and kept going anyway?
  • When was the last time someone on site asked if you were actually okay and meant it?

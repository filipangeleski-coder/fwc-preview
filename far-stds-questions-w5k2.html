Straight questions

The full bank for the ute-cabin videos (STDS): 85 questions, every one about life on the tools, plus a short list of broader ones at the bottom. Pick one. Don't say the question on camera, start on your first real line. Leave the silences in. If it gets too much, shake your head and stop, that's a take too. Only say what you've actually lived. The one that set the bar was the alarm one: 78,670 views, because it named a moment every tradie has had and nobody says out loud.

Your body

  • When you sit down in the ute at knock-off and your lower back makes that noise, what actually goes through your head?
  • That first step out of bed on a site morning, with your heels already sore before the day has started. Have they always felt like that?
  • There is a point in the afternoon where your feet go numb. You know the one. When does it happen for you?
  • Have you ever stretched your toes inside your boots and felt nothing?
  • Have you ever put your boots on and the damp from yesterday is still inside?
  • Monday morning, first lift of the day. What is the first part of your body that tells you it is Monday?
  • Your hands shake after a big day on the jackhammer. You pretend they do not. How long has that been going on?
  • You wake up before the alarm and your body is already tense, before you have even thought about site. What is that?
  • Have you ever stood up after smoko and your knees just do not work for the first few steps?
  • That ache behind your kneecaps by Wednesday. Is that new or has it always been there?

The head stuff

  • Have you ever sat in the ute for ten minutes before going in because you just needed the silence?
  • Have you ever caught yourself working out how long till knock-off before smoko has even happened?
  • Have you ever pulled up at home after the drive from site and realised your jaw has been clenched the whole way?
  • The alarm goes off on a site day and you do not move. Just lie there, knowing what the day is. What is happening in your head in those seconds?

What nobody asks about

  • Does anyone in your life actually know what your feet look like at the end of a week?
  • Have you ever gone to the chemist for your feet and not told anyone?
  • When did you last take a day off site because your body was done? Not sick, just done.
  • Has a physio or chiro ever looked at you and said something about what the tools have done to your body that genuinely scared you?
  • Has anyone at home said something about the way you walk when you come in from site?
  • Have you ever Googled something about your back, knees or feet at 11pm after a shift and not liked what you found?

What the job has cost you

  • Is there something on site you used to do easy, carrying, climbing, getting down on your knees, that you have quietly stopped being able to do?
  • When you look at an older bloke on site, his hands, his back, his knees, do you see yourself in 20 years?
  • Have you ever been out on the weekend and someone noticed you were stiff or limping from the week, and you had to explain it?
  • You know when a young bloke starts on site and he is bouncing around like nothing hurts yet. What do you actually think when you see that?
  • Do you ever look at blokes in offices and think about what their body feels like at 5pm?
  • Is there a day on site you can point to where you realised this trade was going to cost your body more than you planned?
  • Before you started this trade, what did you think your body would feel like at 35? What does it actually feel like?
  • When you signed up, what did you think the hardest part would be? What turned out to be the actual hardest part?
  • What did you think a knock-off beer would feel like before you started? What does it actually feel like now?
  • Day 1 apprentice you, what did he think the boss would be like? What did the boss actually turn out to be?
  • What did you think you would do for fun on weekends when you started this trade? What do weekends actually look like?

Mornings

  • Think back to your apprentice years. Is there a version of you that woke up without anything hurting?
  • The first five minutes on site, ute door closed, before the work starts. What does your body do?
  • 5:30am, still dark, the drive to site ahead of you. What do you tell yourself to actually get out of bed?

The silence

  • Is there something you have wanted to say to someone on site but did not, because you would have to admit something?
  • When did you last tell anyone, even one of the crew, how your body really feels after a week on site?
  • Smoko, and someone hands you a pen and says write down every part of you that hurts right now. How long is the list?

The heavy ones (one per filming, max)

  • Is there a kind of tired from this job that a night's sleep, or even a weekend, does not fix?
  • Do you ever walk in the door after site and feel like the day came in with you?
  • What do you do with a bad day on site, where does it actually go?
  • Have you ever been on site and felt completely switched off, not tired, just gone?
  • Is there a point in the work week where your patience runs out and someone at home cops it first?
  • Have you ever kept working through a really bad mental day on site because you could not work out how to say it to the crew, the boss, anyone?
  • Is there a job you are still carrying that you have never actually put down?
  • Do you ever feel like the bloke on site and the bloke at home are two different people?
  • What does a week on the tools do to you by Friday that you spend the whole weekend trying to undo?
  • Have you ever pulled up to the site entrance and just had to sit there for a bit before walking in?
  • Think of who you were before you started on the tools. Is there a bit of that bloke the job took?
  • Is there something you do to get through the heavy weeks on site that you have never told anyone about?

Deeper (only once you are already somewhere real)

  • Do blokes on your crew ever admit when they are in pain? What actually happens?
  • When did you stop expecting to feel good at knock-off?
  • What would you do if you physically could not do this job anymore?
  • Is there a job you finished that you are proud of and have never said so to the crew?
  • What would you say to a young bloke who is just starting out and already feeling it?
  • What keeps you showing up to site, on the mornings you really do not want to?
  • How long does your body have left in this trade?
  • Have you ever driven home from site so cooked you genuinely did not know how you made it?
  • How many times this week, standing on site, have you thought this is not worth it and kept going anyway?
  • When was the last time someone on site asked if you were actually okay and meant it?

Sentence starters (read it out, finish it)

  • The thing I will never admit on site is...
  • My feet have not felt right since...
  • I do not tell the crew but by 2pm on site I...
  • The thing that scares me about doing this until I am 50 is...
  • I can feel my age in my ___ when I climb out of the ute at knock-off.
  • The 5am starts are getting harder because...
  • I have hidden how much pain I am in from the crew, from the boss, from...
  • On site I pretend I do not notice when...
  • My body started telling me something was wrong on the tools when...
  • The drive home from site is the only time I...
  • The part of working on site I will never get used to is...
  • I know it is going to be a bad day on site when...
  • The worst part of the trade is not the work, it is...

Lionel only

  • When you keep working the run past the point you should have taken a day off, what does that actually feel like in your body?
  • Is there something about council work that the blokes on private sites just do not understand?
  • You deal with what people throw away every day. Does that change how you think about things?
  • When did you last take a proper day off the run, and did you even want to?
  • Is there a point in a long run where you stop feeling tired and just go numb?
  • What does your body feel like on the first morning of a proper day off, no early start, no run?
  • Is there something the job gives you that you could not explain to someone who has never worked a run?

Grant and Lionel, same question, filmed separately

  • Knowing what this trade does to a body, would you take the job again from day one?
  • Coming home from site, is this job making you a better or worse bloke to be around?
  • Have you ever really talked to anyone, a mate on site, the missus, anyone, about what this job is doing to your body and your head?
  • Is there a day on the job that nearly broke you?
  • What do you actually think when someone on site says they are struggling?
  • What do you wish the people at home understood about a day on site that they just do not get?

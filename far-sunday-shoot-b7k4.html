Before Sunday

Fil By Saturday night

  • Whose land is it? Not council. Not anyone's employer. No site sign, no company ute, no logo in any shot. If nobody can say whose it is, we do not film there. This is exactly how we lost four videos and about eight million views in July.
  • Confirm the actual name of the spot and send it to the group.
  • Releases. One page each, plain English, that says you are happy for FAR to use your face and voice. Four printed copies, signed on the tailgate before the first take. Nobody has ever signed one.
  • Load the ute. Shovel, string line, laser level, something that looks like a pipe locator, tape, saw and an offcut, spirit level, one board or sheet, a drill with a battery, esky with a pie and a packet of chips, a coffee cup, four plain hi-vis. Boots: a pair of V2s, one properly dead pair, one never-worn old pair. Tripod, chargers, phone storage cleared.

Rules for every take

  • Hook is shot one. Film the opening moment first and film it hardest.
  • Two takes minimum. One as written, one loose. We use the real one.
  • No selling, no brand names, no logos, no "check us out". Ever.
  • Real sound. No music on the day.
  • Whoever is filming says the clip name out loud before every take. "Micromanager, take two."
  • Shoot enough for a 30 to 50 second cut. The edit trims. Do not rush the beats.
  • Second phone on the tripod for the wide on every group piece.

1. The Micromanager refilm

GrantLionel+ one

Same video, new ground. Do not make it cleverer. The shovel grab is the opening. The job is finished and cleaned up. The boss walks over, says nothing, crouches, checks the back. "Yeah... nah... yeah." "Who done the back?" "Do it again." Walks off. The veteran, coffee in hand, never looks up: "He does that." Beat. "It's good, by the way."

The line that stays: "Even when he's gone he's not."

Posts Tuesday 15 Sep. It did 2.9 million views before it was archived.

2. The Old Fella, part two respect pass

Old fellaYoung bloke+ one in the background

The original got pulled by Instagram and the appeal lost. We do not know which bit tripped it. So: same performance, one new beat, and a new ending, so this is a part two and not a re-upload.

  • Keep the opening. Young bloke shoves a laser level in his face. He does not look at it, already lining the wall by eye with string. "Put that away. I've been doing this longer than you've been alive."
  • Keep the wall by eye (dead plumb) and the measure by thumb (cuts perfect).
  • New beat. The young bloke's laser says the wall is out by two mil. Old fella, still not looking up: "The laser's wrong." Young bloke checks the laser against the string. The laser was wrong.
  • Film the ending both ways. A: he walks off muttering, like the original. B: the young bloke looks at the laser, looks at the wall, puts the laser back in the ute and picks up the string. No line. We pick in the edit.
  • He is the bloke who is never wrong. He is never the joke.

Posts Friday 18 Sep. The original was our biggest ever, about 4.8 million.

7. The Long Goodbye bonus, last

Two+ a walk-past

Needs the ute and the light changing, so it goes last if at all. Open mid-goodbye: engine running, door swung open, keys spinning on a finger, arm on the door frame, mid-story. Cut to the trapped mate's dead-flat face holding a drill he stopped using forty minutes ago. Same framing while the light changes. A third bloke walks past with tools, again in a clean shirt, again with a fishing rod. He finally gets in, door shuts, relief. Reverse lights. "One more thing." Cut back to the exact same dead face.

Getting more people to the link

FAR · 19 Oct 2026 · what the big brands and our own numbers say, and seven things to try for four weeks

Vote here before Saturday: the poll on the dashboard. One tap per move. Add a note if you'd change it.

None of the ten product brands asks for a follow inside its funny reels: 8 of 783 captions ask, and 1 of 110 watched reels gives a reason to follow. They keep the reach reel clean and put the ask somewhere else: a founder post, a carousel, the profile, a Story link, a caption line with a reason.

Our own numbers say the same thing. The founder-to-camera reels that ended on the Founding Crew ask made our number 1, 2 and 6 website-click days of 2026. Founder reels earn 3.4 follows per 1,000 views, against 0.5 for other reels with the same small reach.

And sign-ups follow link taps, not follows. Over 33 weeks, sign-ups line up with link taps (0.55 correlation) and barely with follows (0.11). The Old Fella fortnight brought about 2,370 follows and 15 sign-ups. About 9 in 100 people who tap the bio link sign up, in good weeks and quiet ones.

So the job for the first 100 is more taps on the link, not more followers. The skits keep bringing strangers in. A founder, as himself, tells them who we are and where to go.

The seven moves

1

A founder to camera every Monday, ending on the spoken Founding Crew ask

30 to 50 s, phone on a tripod, live voice, no music needed. He says his name and FAR in the first five seconds, shows one real thing about the boot that is still open (the 500 being made, the boot as it stands, a call not yet made) and what it is costing him, then ends on the spoken ask the way DW8fWioEgFw and DWqXvE7B8S- end ('join the Founding Crew, link in bio').

Who: The founder on screen (Fil W44, Lionel W45, Grant W46, Con W47); the week owner posts it. Fallback if the week's founder has not filmed by Saturday night: Fil films.
Time: about 60 minutes a week
When: W44 to W47: Mon 26 Oct, Mon 2 Nov, Mon 9 Nov, Mon 16 Nov, 7pm

2

Build every Tuesday and Friday skit on a person type, named on frame 1, with several beats on that one type

Two to four founders in character; frame 1 names the type in 8 words or fewer, the way 'POV: THE MICROMANAGER' and 'WORK NICKNAMES' did; live sound; 30 to 70 s of escalating beats on that one type to a payoff; no brand, no boot pitch, no ask. The type is a behaviour a viewer sends to the bloke he works with, never an age or a group (the Old Fella was removed for hateful conduct).

Who: The week owner organises (Grant W44, Con W45, Lionel W46, Grant W47); Grant and Lionel lead on camera, Fil and Con as cast; Lionel, Fil or the dashboard editor cuts. Fallback if a cast day falls over: a switch or reaction piece from the W43 pool fills the slot that week.

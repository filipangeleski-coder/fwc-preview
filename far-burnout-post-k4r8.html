What changed in v5

  • New opening. Fil slides in and flops on the grass, legs up, freeze: WHAT $1,000 OF BOOTS DOES TO A MAN. Then the tyre on the boot as "what happened". It's the same trick as the pancake reel: someone doing something odd in the first second, and you know what the video is about by second two.
  • It's a loop now. The video starts on the grass and ends on the grass. The last line lands where the first shot left off.
  • Why not a viral clip from someone else: using a stranger's video in a FAR ad needs a licence. Newsflare quotes brands £700 to £20,000 a clip, and the others price on request. Most of those clips are real accidents too. The pancake reel works because her own shot continues the motion of the borrowed clip, and Fil's dive is the only shot we have that can do that.

What changed in v4

  • It breathes. v3 was 17 seconds and felt rushed. v4 adds holds where a line or a graphic needs to land, never gaps between words: the boot freezes mid-air under SOMEONE ELSE DID., a beat after "drain", the receipt stays up over a second after INCL. THE NAME, a beat after "right." and after "in his life."
  • The grass ending is back, with something on it. As Fil says "what you ask them to build", a build order prints over the sky: WORK BOOTS, ORDERED BY: SOME BLOKE (the head-office bloke from earlier), and APPROVED stamps on his last word. It freezes on his last look down the lens.
  • Why not the car-boot ending: the car-boot take is the one Fil redid on the day. He starts it again straight after and swears, and that's why it sounded unfinished.
  • Kept from v3: the new opening (tyre on, boot flat, springs out), THE TYRE DIDN'T KILL THESE. SOMEONE ELSE DID., the receipt, the blurred logo on the crushed boot.

New 1: the switch videos

A stranger's viral clip. At the exact moment it goes wrong, it cuts to one of us doing the same thing. About 10 seconds, no talking.

Why three at once: a clothing brand did this and got 14 million views. They made a second one with a different clip and got 9 million. Same recipe, only the clip changes. Our own refilm of That One Bloke was expected to do 15,000 and is on 424,000. When something works, we make more of it.

▶ Watch the brand that did 14M, then 9M

Same line on screen at the end of all three:

OLD BOOTS DON'T GO QUIETLY.

Caption for all three:

Took them off for good. They didn't take it well.

Rules for all three

  • Old boots only, never the FARs. Turn any brand name on them away from the camera.
  • Soft landing: grass or a mat. Walking pace. One slow practice first. Nothing higher than your knee.
  • Not on a work site. Not on a council footpath or kerb. Backyard, driveway or front porch.
  • No house numbers, street signs, number plates or company utes in frame.
  • Phone vertical. Three takes each. Keep rolling 5 seconds after the fall and stay down a beat.

New 2: the Trial Reels test

Instagram's own test. A Trial Reel goes to people who don't follow us first, and our followers don't see it until we share it. We post the doorbell and the wheelbarrow as trials, see which one strangers like more, and share only the winner.

  1. Tue 7pm: post both. On the last screen before Share, turn on the Trial toggle. Leave the automatic sharing off, because we pick the winner ourselves.
  2. Wed 7pm, 24 hours later: go to the profile. Trial reels sit next to the drafts. Open each one and write down its views and shares.
  3. More views wins. If they're within a fifth of each other, more shares wins.
  4. On the winner, tap Share with everyone. Leave the other one as it is.
  5. Put both sets of numbers in the poll's last question, so we learn from it.

Why not test on TikTok first: nothing shows a TikTok winner is an Instagram winner, and we've only got 910 TikTok followers. Every reel still goes on TikTok too, just not as the test.

New 3: How I feel when

Two or three seconds of you in a real moment with one line on screen, then a hard cut to a famous reaction clip that finishes the joke. A salon owner with under 5,000 followers did exactly this and got 903,000 views.

▶ Watch the salon one

Pick a line (all passed our checks):

HOW MY FEET FEEL AT 2PM IN THE OLD PAIR
HOW MY FEET FEEL WHEN THE BOOTS COME OFF AT KNOCK-OFF
THE OLD PAIR WATCHING ME PUT THE NEW ONES ON
  • The first two are the old boots: you, the old pair, the feeling. The third needs the new pair, so only if you've got the sample pair.
  • The reaction clip: a meme everyone has seen. A collapse, a celebration, a jealous stare. No kids. Crop out any other account's name or logo.
  • Edit in CapCut: your shot with the line (2 to 3 seconds), hard cut to the reaction, 8 to 10 seconds total, keep the clip's own sound.
  • No claims about our boot. Don't say it's comfier, tougher or waterproof. Run the caption through Check a caption on the dashboard before it goes up.

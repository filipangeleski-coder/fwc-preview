Boot filming sheet

Two shots, one boot, about ten minutes. Read it once before you start.
What this is for
These photos and clips feed everything we make. Product shots, the website, ads, the lot. Get these right once and we stop needing to re-shoot.

Setup, same for both shots

  • Grey sheet or a plain grey wall behind the boot. A bed sheet over a door works. No kitchen, no lounge room, nothing patterned.
  • Phone propped on something solid. Books, a box, anything. Not handheld, not even for a second.
  • Landscape, not portrait.
  • Wipe the lens.
  • One light source. A window works. Do not stand between the window and the boot.
  • Nothing else in frame. No hands, no feet, no mugs, no dog.

Shot 1, the spin

  • Boot on the spinning stand, in the middle of the frame.
  • Frame it so the whole boot fits with a bit of room around it. It must not touch the edges at any point in the turn.
  • Start recording, then give it a gentle spin.
  • Let it turn twice, all the way around. Slow is better than fast.
  • Stop recording only after it has finished turning.
The three things that kill this shot
A hand reaching in. The boot drifting out of frame. Anything recognisable behind it.

Shot 2, the worn boot

A boot that has actually been worked. Dust, scuffs, concrete on the sole, creases across the toe.

  • Do not clean it up. The mess is the whole point of this shot.
  • Same grey background, same locked-off phone.
  • Take stills, not video. One photo from each angle below.

Angles, one photo each:

1Side on, zip side facing you
2Side on, plain side facing you
3Front on, toe pointing at the camera
4Back on, so the stamped circle on the heel is visible
5Angled from the front, three quarters on
6Sole facing the camera
7Straight down from above, whole boot in frame
8Close on the toe cap where the rubber meets the leather
Why we need a dirty one
Every photo we own is of a brand new spotless boot. A spotless boot standing on a building site looks fake to anyone who works on one. Without a worn boot to work from we cannot make a believable site photo at all.

When you are done

  • Drop everything straight into the Drive folder. Do not send it through WhatsApp, it wrecks the quality.
  • Do not crop, filter or edit anything.
  • Send Fil a message so he knows it is there.

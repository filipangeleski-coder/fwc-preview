The boot video works

Saturday 29 August. Two jobs for you two, ten minutes with a boot.

What we can do now

We can make proper cinematic videos of our boot. Not stock footage, not a lookalike. Ours.

The test one is a wide shot on a concrete slab, a beam of light coming through the dust, and the camera moving in one smooth run until it is right on the toe cap. Ten seconds. Every frame is our actual boot. The ridged toe cap, the black panels either side of the zip, the velcro tab, the black eyelets, the tan sole. All correct, start to finish.

We also got a top-down shot we have never had, and a full spin of the boot.

The one that went wrong, and why

First spin came out with a blank heel. No stamped circle logo on the back.

Reason was simple. We never showed the computer a photo of the heel, so it had nothing to copy and it guessed. Added the photo, logo came back correct.

Whole night of testing cost about 230 credits out of 1,377.

Two jobs, same ten minutes

Grant or Lionel Job one, the spin

  • Boot on the spinning stand.
  • Grey sheet or a plain grey wall behind it. Not the kitchen.
  • Phone propped on something solid. Not held.
  • Let it turn twice, all the way around.
  • No hands in the shot at any point.

We have a spin already but there is a hand in it, a kitchen behind it, and the boot wanders out of frame halfway through. Cannot use it.

Grant or Lionel Job two, a boot that has been worked

  • A boot with real wear on it. Dust, scuffs, concrete on the sole.
  • Same angles as job one.
  • Do not clean it up first. The mess is the point.
Why this one matters
Every photo we own is of a spotless boot. A spotless boot standing on a building site looks fake to anyone who actually works on one. Right now we cannot make a believable site photo because we have nothing worn to work from.

Also sorted this week

Your photos and videos were spread across eleven different places. There is now one page that answers where anything is. Boot photos, filming footage, logos, covers, captions.

Two folders turned up that nobody knew about. One has photos of actual boot faults, insoles coming apart, a cut sole. The other has our insole side by side with two competitors. Both are exactly the sort of proof we should be showing people, and both were sitting there invisible.

We also found the boot reference photos existed in only one spot. If that folder had gone, we would have lost the ability to make any of this. Three copies now.

What the cushion is for

Five costs are still genuinely unknown, and all five can only move against us:

  • Freight, because no forwarder is appointed yet. The range is $2,262 wide.
  • Import duty, $1,271 if any supplier's certificate of origin is wrong.
  • A certificate update on the boot, $1,067 if we take it.
  • The box price, still a placeholder, $560 across the run.
  • The exchange rate. A five cent move costs us about $1,500 on the China orders.
What we commit to
That money covers cost blowouts on the lines above and nothing else. Not marketing, not travel. Whatever we do not spend gets paid straight back off the principal.

The honest version
At three in ten we can pay your interest but not the principal that year. At half we are comfortable on interest and starting to build. We would rather tell you that now than have you find it out in month eight.

The reason the principal timing matters: the second production run gets ordered while the first one is still selling. The factory needs three to four months. If we hand the principal back inside year one there is no second run, and the business stops after one batch. On the agreement we have both got, that is already how it works, so we are confirming it rather than asking for it.

What we need from you

  1. Is the 10% per year, or 10% once? The agreement reads like it accrues annually, which over two years is $14,000 rather than $7,000. We would rather have that settled in writing now than assume either way.
  2. Can you confirm the term is two years?
  3. Is the lender your super fund, or you personally? If it is the fund, we understand it has to be on proper commercial terms and your auditor needs to see that. A two year loan at a set rate where the borrower schedules the repayments is exactly that, so we do not think there is a conflict, but you should check it with whoever does your fund.
  4. Who does your side want as guarantor? The agreement has a person guaranteeing, not the company. That is a conversation the three of us need to have before anyone signs, so we are not answering it today.
  5. Do you want any security over it, or is the guarantee enough?
  6. Does your accountant need anything from us? Happy to provide whatever makes it clean at your end.

Risks, stated plainly

  • We have not sold a boot yet. No trading history, no proof anyone will pay this price. That is the real risk and no number on this page removes it.
  • The freight is not quoted. Three quotes are being chased. It is the largest number we cannot yet confirm.
  • The factory may want the full balance before shipping rather than the usual part now, part later. If so, the money goes out earlier than planned. The cushion is partly there for that.
  • If the boots do not sell, the stock is still worth something but it is not cash, and it would take longer to get you back than two years.
And the thing worth saying out loud
This is a mate lending into a business with no track record. If it goes badly, it is your money and it is our friendship. We have thought about that. We would rather you said no than said yes to be nice.

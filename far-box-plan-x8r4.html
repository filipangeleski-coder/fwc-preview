The FAR Box

The magnetic unboxing box for the 500 run: the concept, the proof nobody else does it, real suppliers and prices, and the one trap in the plan. Built 31 Aug 2026 from a sourced research run. Every price here is a market figure, not a quote.

The concept

A rigid box that opens from the front with a magnetic close. Boxes are the one thing with a 100% open rate, so ours has to be worth filming.

  • The experience we are chasing: the adidas NMD Pitch Black kit, opens flat, shoes set into foam one side, the extras set into foam pockets on the other. Ours will not be a briefcase, but that feeling, everything in its place when the lid swings open, is the target. Whether the socks and bag sit in a formed tray inside the box is a design-session call.
  • Finish: UV print on the box, gloss FAR marks on matte black. Stays monochrome, catches light on camera.
  • Cover: the FAR wordmark, tagline under it. Wording not locked yet, candidates are on the table.
  • Inside the lid: the founder story with a photo of the three of us. The customer reads it with the boots still in the box.

Nobody does this

Checked BRUNT, Thursday, Tecovas, Red Wing and Blundstone through unboxing videos and reviews: every one ships standard printed cardboard. Steel Blue shows no packaging anywhere. The only boot-scale magnetic box footwear has ever seen is the Nike Air Mag, a limited edition, never a production box. A magnetic front-opening boot box would be a first among boot brands. That claim held up to a real check, with one honest limit: we checked the big names and swept broadly, not every boot brand on earth.

The reference boxes

  • Nike Air Mag box, boot scale, magnet sealed, foam interior. This is the box.
  • Johnnie Walker Blue Label, the front door with a magnetic catch and a printed interior. The door mechanic.
  • FoldaBox XL Deep, a stock folding magnetic front-flap box at nearly boot size (35 x 35 x 15 cm). About USD 20 delivered as a sample.
The 20 dollar move
Order one FoldaBox before any factory gets an email. Holding it answers the biggest open question, whether a folding magnetic box feels premium enough, or whether we need the solid-wall version.

The one trap

Rigid boxes ship already built.
500 boot-sized empty boxes take up about 10 cubic metres, more room on the ship than the boots themselves. The way out exists: folding magnetic rigid boxes that pack flat, cut the shipping space roughly 10x, and cost about USD 2 to 3 more per box. The catch is we assemble them here, a couple of minutes each, which fits the call already made that packing happens in Australia.

Two ways to route it, decided after the samples and Don's answer:

  • Built boxes to the boot factory, boots packed into them there, everything ships together. Simplest, but the boxes must reach Qingdao inside the 60 day production window, and the shipment gets bigger and heavier.
  • Flat boxes straight to us, boots arrive in plain factory boxes, we box and pack everything here. Kills the deadline and the shipping bulk, adds the re-boxing work, gives us eyes on every single pair.

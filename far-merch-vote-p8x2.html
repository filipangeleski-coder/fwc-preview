How it works

  • Most votes wins. A tie goes to the cheaper answer.
  • Your votes from Sunday are shown under each question. Vote again here. This one is the one that counts.
  • A yes buys that item at up to the price shown. If the real order comes out dearer, it comes back to all four of us.
  • Dollars are Australian unless it says US$. "Landed" means with freight to Wollongong. Anything marked estimate is our maths, not a factory's quote.

The case against

  • The box already holds nine things: boots, socks, bag, two cards, stickers, air freshener, the safety leaflet and the numbered tag. Nobody's arguing the unboxing doesn't matter. The question is what the tenth and eleventh things add, and nobody has a number on that.
  • It isn't free. A stubby and a keychain together cost about $2.80 for every one we hand out, about $1,700 all up.
  • The keychain is the thing most likely to end up in the bin. Fil says so too. There's no number either way.
  • Nobody has a list of who gets the tees and caps. Without one, 100 of each sits in a garage. If each of the 50 free pairs goes out with a tee and a cap, 50 is gone on day one.
  • Sticker glue matters. Solvent glue attacks hard hat plastic. A FAR sticker that weakens someone's hard hat is the worst look a safety boot brand could have. Whoever prints them has to put the glue in writing.

How it's built
Our real FAR wordmark file, untouched, with WE REFUSE TO ACCEPT IT set underneath in Space Grotesk Bold, our headline font. The words are turned into shapes, so a factory can embroider or print it without having our font. No full stop inside the logo. It's a proper vector file, the kind a design studio hands over, not an AI picture.
Picked: A, on everything (Fil, 11 Oct). The file went to all six merch factories at 8am with a request for a digital proof.

The three options

A. Full width my pick for caps

The tagline runs the full width of FAR, lined up with the F and the R. Big enough to embroider: on a cap, stitched letters need to be about 5mm tall to read, and this is the only option that gets there.

B. Centred

Smaller and centred. The weakest of the three: not as strong as A, not as clean as C.

C. Spaced out print only

Small letters, wide spacing. The most premium look, the way big brands sign off. Too small to stitch, so it only works printed: the back of a tee, a stubby holder.

My recommendation
A everywhere, so FAR always looks the same. Or A on caps and C on anything printed, if you want the print to feel more premium.

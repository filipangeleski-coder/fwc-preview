Site File 001

The Honeymoon Phase. A new kind of video we're testing. Have a look and tell Fil what you reckon.

What it is

The "taking care of FAR boots" footage from the 13 Sep shoot, cut like a police case file. Typed notes, boxes that snap onto the boots, a CASE CLOSED stamp. It's the first test of moving text and graphics on a video, which we'd banned until now.

The times on screen are the real times off the phone. The real order turned out funnier than the plan:

10:04New pair belted into the front seat.
10:10Washed with drinking water. Bagged.
10:14Goes down, still wearing the old pair. A boot flies off.
10:17Old pair run over.
10:18Old pair thrown in the scrub.

Tell Fil, one line each

  • Funny, or not?
  • Does it look like FAR, or like a template anyone could make?
  • Would you want more of these, say one a week from footage we've already shot?
  • Anything you'd change: the words, the speed, music under it?
Fil's first read: "very different, for a first one shot, it's pretty good."

How we sell the first 500

Eight calls for the three of you, from a long think with Fil on 27 Sep about why people are not buying yet. Each one has the numbers and our read. Nothing here is decided until you three say so.

What the numbers say first

  • Nobody has ever been asked to pay FAR a dollar. Every number we have is a free sign-up number.
  • About 126 people are on the list. 25 have told us a boot size. 7 people have ever referred a mate.
  • The three biggest reels, close to 900,000 views between them, brought in about 12 sign-ups. People sign up when someone they know asks them. They do not sign up off a reel.
  • Each of us agreed on 22 Aug to message 20 to 50 of our own people. Nobody has sent one yet.
  • Dave's money covers everything to launch. We do not need pre-sale money to pay for the boots. Selling early is about Christmas and the other crowd launching in October, not survival.

Call 1: $219 for the first 100 sold, or the first 150?

Fil's read on 27 Sep: 100 sold at $219, and 50 pairs kept back to give away as seeds. Two days earlier the site went live saying "first 150", on the understanding the three of you had settled on 150. The site is wrong until this is confirmed.

Our read
100 sold plus 50 seeds. It matches the box promise (first 100 get the full box) and the welcome email that already says 100. Say the number once and the site gets fixed. All three

Call 2: the 30-day guarantee runs from the day the boots land

The site said "30 days from the day you buy them" (superseded: the clock now runs from the day the boots are received, Fil 27 Sep). Boots land around Christmas. Anyone who pays in October or November would have a guarantee that runs out before the box arrives. Fil has already called it: the clock starts when the boots are received.

Our read
Minute it and move on. It is the one thing on the site that lets a stranger buy without a mate vouching, so it should be the headline, not the fine print. All three

Call 3: sell the first 100 before the boots land, or from the garage with boots in hand?

Fil voted for opening the pre-sale when the factory finishes in late November. Lionel voted for no pre-sale, launch with boots in hand. Grant has not voted. Fil's air-freight idea below changes this: if 150 pairs fly, they land about 10 Dec, so the first 100 can sell with boots in the country before Christmas either way.

  • Pre-sale first: three weeks of earlier money and a read on demand. Needs a terms page and a refund page (both missing today) and a lawyer to read them before the first dollar, which was agreed in July and never done.
  • Boots in hand first: the guarantee works from day one, no money taken for a promise, and the warm names get "landed this morning, yours ships today".
Our read
Boots in hand, with the 150 by air. The pre-sale only wins if the terms page question is answered first. All three
New on 28 Sep
Fil now backs the pre-sale, for a reason the page did not have: if 150 fly and 350 come by sea, the 150 on the plane are the first 100 buyers plus the 50 seeds. We do not know the buyers' sizes until they commit. A pre-sale before about 20 Nov tells us exactly which sizes to put on the plane. Flying all 500 would remove the guess too, and the freight quotes will say what that costs. The terms and refund pages are still needed before anyone pays. Lionel and Grant, this one is yours to answer. Grant Lionel

Call 4: 150 pairs by air, and the seeds go out after a wear check

Don will not release boots early, but will put 150 on a plane when the run finishes on 28 Nov. Air is 8 to 12 days and roughly $2,600 to $3,300 plus a second customs entry. The other 350 come by sea and land late Dec to early Jan.

The catch: nobody has appointed a freight forwarder. The three quote requests are written and have never been sent. No forwarder, no air quote, no plane.

Our read
Yes to the plane. The four of you wear production pairs for a week before the 50 seeds go out, because these are the first boots on the new leather anyone has worn, and a creator's unboxing is the worst place to find a problem. Forwarder quotes go this week. Fil

Call 5: the 50 seed pairs, who gets them

Fil's rule: creators who are still on the tools and have built a following, like Evan La Motta, not random influencers. We already have a written plan from March with five gates: Australian, working a trade now, 10K to 100K followers, real tradie comments not bots, no boot-brand deal. It also has a second lane: site foremen, because a respected foreman in FAR boots reaches his whole site as a mate.

  • A tracker is being built on the dashboard so all four of us can add names, handles, trades and sizes. Cap of 50. You three approve, Con can nominate.
  • A gifted pair that ends up in a post has to be said out loud. It is the law, and it is in the plan.
Our read
Both lanes. Set the split between creators and foremen. Grant and Lionel are on sites every day, so the foreman lane is theirs. Fil works the creators. All three

Call 6: a draw at delivery for people who post their box

Fil's idea: when your boots arrive, post them to your story to go into a draw. The buyer's crew sees it, we reshare it. That is the mate-to-mate channel, which is how tradies actually buy.

  • The share object is the box or the numbered card, not an order screenshot. The box is designed to be photographed and it pays off "there's more in the box". A screenshot is ugly and carries an address.
  • The prize is ours: boots for your crew, five pairs, about $450 at cost. That keeps the pool under $3,000, which needs no permit in any state. Not a tool brand's prize. We are the brand people advertise, not the other way round.
  • "Entered to win" in the post says the incentive out loud, so nobody is being misled.
  • Needs the terms page, same one the pre-sale needs.
Our read
Yes, in that shape. Runs when boots exist, not before. All three

Call 7: the referral ladder, keep and fix or kill

On 8 Sep the room did not like it. Since then the numbers came in: it is the only thing that has ever moved sign-ups outside launch week, and one bloke has already earned the free pair. The rewards (socks, slides) are things the first 100 get in the box anyway, so they mean nothing to a buyer.

Our read
Keep it and fix it: rewards that are not already in the box, a cap on the free-boots rung, and check that the 10-referral earner's mates are real. Or kill it cleanly and tell the bloke who earned a pair. Not the current state, which is a live offer nobody backs. All three

Call 8: who gets asked to pay first

The plan so far has been: email the list, then post about it. The list is the weakest thing we have. Three full emails ever sent, three clicks, zero sales. The strongest ask is personal and by name.

Our read
Each of us to our own 20 to 50 first, with a price, a real date and a guarantee that works. The list email the same night. The Monday episode third. Measured by who sent what in week one. Fil Grant Lionel

The shipping plan

We appoint one Australian freight forwarder who does the whole chain door to door: picks up from all three suppliers in China, combines it into one consignment, ships it, clears customs, and trucks it to us. One shipment, one customs entry, instead of three of everything. That saves roughly AUD 550 to 1,050 in duplicated broker and government fees (our estimate).

Called by Fil 31 Aug, sing out if you disagree
Boots get packed into their retail boxes at the factory, but everything else, the founder note, the numbered-boot cards, socks, bag, stickers, goes in here in Australia, packed by us. The first 100 boxes need handwriting and numbering anyway, we get eyes on every pair before it reaches a customer after what happened with V1, and the magnetic box opens and closes clean so nothing looks touched. Factory packing becomes the right answer at reorder scale, not this run.

Three forwarders get quote requests first: Explorate (Australian, online, full cost breakdown), International Cargo Express (Sydney, own customs brokers, good with first-time importers), and Freightos (instant online marketplace, keeps the other two honest). Every request asks the same four things: combined pickup from our three suppliers, the fastest direct sea service, the air option on the first 150, and delivery cost to Wollongong.

Paperwork and rules

The one that could bite: the label law.
Australian law has footwear-specific import rules. Every imported pair must carry a durable, securely attached label stating country of origin plus what the sole, the upper and the lining are made of. Border Force can seize stock that does not comply. Our tag drawing for Estar was already being made for AU sizes, the composition wording now goes on it too, checked by the customs broker before production starts. Caught 29 Aug, nobody had it on a list anywhere.
  • 0% duty needs a Certificate of Origin from every supplier. Not just Estar. Newway and CJ each need one too, or their part of the shipment pays 5%.
  • GST deferral. There is a scheme that lets the border GST be paid through the monthly BAS instead of cash on arrival. Needs a switch to monthly BAS first. Accountant question, already on the list.
  • Importer of record. FAR WORKWEAR PTY LTD's ABN is all we need, the broker handles the rest. One rule: never accept a forwarder's "DDP, we handle everything" offer, that makes them the importer and we lose the 0% duty and the GST credit.
  • Packing instruction to all three suppliers: no straw, no raw timber packing, any pallets heat-treated and stamped. Contamination is what gets shipments held at biosecurity.

Where it physically lands

Fifty-odd cartons and 900 kilos is a truck with a tailgate, not a courier van. Three options, decided together with the packing call:

  • Residential delivery to Keiraville. Works, carries tailgate and hand-unload surcharges, and it is 900 kg into a garage by hand.
  • Pick up from the forwarder's Sydney depot ourselves. Cheapest, but multiple trips or a hired truck, and storage fees start about 3 days after it clears.
  • A storage unit near Wollongong as the landing spot and packing base. Roughly AUD 80 to 300 a month for the size we need (Jul 2026 listing prices). Catch: most storage places will not sign for a freight delivery, so this only works with one that accepts booked deliveries.

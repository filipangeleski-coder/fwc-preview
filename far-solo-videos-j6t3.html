Your solo videos

Three each for Grant and Lionel, filmed at home. These are the same cards that are on the dashboard Ideas board. They've been on hold since 14 September while Fil read them, and he gives the go at the meeting. They're a guide, not a script: change any line, beat or ending so it's how you'd actually say it.

The one rule

The first shot is the video. Film it on its own, three or four times, harder each time. If frame 1 wouldn't make YOU stop with the sound off, don't film the rest yet.

  • Phone propped, not held. Hi-vis in the first shot.
  • Real sound first, no music.
  • No "follow us", no brand named, no other brand's logo in shot (tape it).
  • 30 to 50 seconds. Longer only if it earns it.
  • When it's done: put the file on the business Drive under 2 Social media > 02 Filming days - Raw, in a folder with the date and your name. Then tap "filmed" on the card.

Grant

1. Knock-off test

You in the FAR boots, filmed at knock-off after a real day.

First shot

The hop off the tailgate, mid-air. Film it first, three or four times.

Text on screen
10 HOURS ON CONCRETE. WATCH.
First thing you say
"Ten hours on concrete. Watch this."
Beats
  1. The hop or jog off the tailgate.
  2. Boots off, sock off, show your foot: no sock ring dug into it.
  3. Say what's true after your real day: what hurt and what didn't, one sentence. If something hurt, say so.
  4. Drop the boots on the tray, thud, walk off.

Flat, honest, tired. It's you telling it straight, not an ad. Don't say "no break-in", "comfiest" or anything about water. We can't back those yet.

2. The insole

Any dead boot from your shed. Your own idea from the board, script Fil already OK'd.

First shot

The insole torn in half with your bare hands, one rip. Film the rip first, four times.

Text on screen
YOU'VE BEEN LIED TO ABOUT YOUR BOOTS
First thing you say
"You've been lied to about what's inside a 250 dollar boot."
Beats
  1. The rip.
  2. Hold up the halves and say it the smoko way. They know the one in there is rubbish. Why would they put a good one in, when you're going to go sort it yourself for another fifty bucks?
  3. If you've got the insole you bought to replace it, hold them side by side.
  4. Both halves in the bin.

Your opinion, said flat, not a rant. No brand named or showing (tape the tag).

3. One straight question (STDS)

Ute cabin, shoulders up, phone propped, no music. Pick ONE question from the list at the bottom. Open on your first line of the answer, never on the question. Leave the silences in. If it gets too much, shake your head and stop. Never a "follow us" on these.

Lionel

1. Shed pair

You need two pairs: one you wore to death, and one old pair that sat in the shed and never got worn.

First shot

Both boots held up together. Film that first.

Text on screen
ONE DID 900 SHIFTS. ONE DID NONE. (use your real number)
First thing you say
"One of these did 900 shifts. The other did zero. Guess which one's dead."
Beats
  1. Both boots up.
  2. Bend the WORN one: still fine.
  3. Bend the NEVER-WORN one: it cracks, and the sole crumbles.
  4. "It's called hydrolysis." Boots that sit in the shed die anyway.
  5. Put them down, done.

Let the crack do the work, don't explain it. Nothing about the FAR sole. That one waits on paperwork.

2. Bad advice

First shot

You walking AT the camera, already talking.

Text on screen
YOU'VE PROBABLY GIVEN THIS ADVICE
First thing you say
"The worst advice in construction gets handed out every single day. You've probably said it."
Beats
  1. The advice, one line each, the way it's said on site: they'll wear in. Harden up. It's just the job. She'll be right.
  2. What each one cost you or a mate: the knee, the heel, the weeks off. Your real ones.
  3. The turn: it's not the bloke, it's the boot.
  4. Last line: "Next time you hear it, look down."

Deadpan. No lecture, you're one of them. Nothing medical you can't back with your own body.

3. One straight question (STDS)

Same as Grant's: ute cabin, one question from the list below, open on your answer, leave the silences in.

The straight questions (pick one)

  1. What's the injury you never told your missus about?
  2. What's the closest you've come to not making it home from a job?
  3. What's the one job you still think about at 2am?
  4. How many mates have you lost to this industry, and what do you tell yourself about it?
  5. What did the doctor say that you decided to ignore?
  6. What's the thing your body does now that you hide from the young blokes?
  7. When did you last cry about work, and where were you?
  8. What have you never said to the bloke who taught you?
  9. What do you say when someone asks how work was, and what's the real answer?
  10. What's the closest you've come to walking off site for good?

Or your own. Only say what you've actually lived.

Your solo videos

Five scripts. Grant and Lionel, pick any 3 each and film them at home. They were first written with one of you in mind, but any of them works for either of you. Each one has the lines to say. The bits in [brackets] are yours: your real number, your real story. Only say what you've actually lived. Change any line so it sounds like you. The same scripts are on the dashboard Ideas board.

The one rule

The first shot is the video. Film it on its own, three or four times, harder each time. If frame 1 wouldn't make YOU stop with the sound off, don't film the rest yet.

  • Phone propped, not held. Hi-vis in the first shot.
  • Real sound first, no music.
  • No "follow us", no brand named, no other brand's logo in shot (tape it).
  • 30 to 50 seconds. Longer only if it earns it.
  • When it's done: put the file on the business Drive under 2 Social media > 02 Filming days - Raw, in a folder with the date and your name. Then tap "filmed" on the card.

First written for Grant (either of you can pick them)

1. Knock-off test

You in the FAR boots, filmed at knock-off after a real day.

First shot

The hop off the tailgate, mid-air. Film it first, three or four times.

Text on screen
10 HOURS ON CONCRETE. WATCH.
The script

"Ten hours on concrete. Watch this."

Sit on the tailgate, pull a boot off.

"This is normally the bit where I find out what the day did to me."

Sock off, foot to the camera.

"[Say what you see. No sock ring dug in? Say that. If there is one, say that.]"

"Usually by now my [heels / arches / the balls of my feet] are cooked."

"Today? [One true sentence. What hurt, what didn't.]"

Drop both boots on the tray. Thud.

"Same again tomorrow."

Walk off.

Flat, honest, tired. It's you telling it straight, not an ad. Don't say "no break-in", "comfiest" or anything about water. We can't back those yet.

2. The insole

Any dead boot from your shed. This one was your idea from the board.

First shot

The insole torn in half with your bare hands, one rip. Film the rip first, four times.

Text on screen
YOU'VE BEEN LIED TO ABOUT YOUR BOOTS
The script

"You've been lied to about what's inside a 250 dollar boot."

Hold up the two halves.

"That's it. That's what's under your foot for ten hours a day."

"Every bloke I know bins it and buys a proper one for another fifty bucks."

"So why's the one in a 250 dollar boot this bad?"

If you've got the insole you bought to replace it, hold it next to the halves.

"Two hundred and fifty for the boot. Fifty more for the bit that actually touches your foot."

Drop both halves in the bin.

"[Your own last word. Keep it short.]"

Your opinion, said flat, not a rant. No brand named or showing (tape the tag).

3. One straight question

Ute cabin, shoulders up, phone propped, no music. Pick ONE question from the list below or the full list of 90. Don't say the question on camera. Start on your first real line, the one that comes out when you think of the moment.

Rough shape if you need one: the moment (where you were, what happened), what you did or didn't say, where it sits now. Leave the silences in. If it gets too much, shake your head and stop. That's a take too. Never a "follow us" on these.

First written for Lionel (either of you can pick them)

1. Shed pair

Two of YOUR old pairs, never FAR boots: one you wore to death, and one that sat in the shed and never got worn. This only works if the never-worn pair has a foam sole. Bend it off camera first. If it doesn't crack, don't film it.

First shot

Both boots held up together. Film that first.

Text on screen
ONE DID [900] SHIFTS. ONE DID NONE. (your real number)
The script

"One of these did [900] shifts. The other did zero. Guess which one's dead."

Bend the worn one. It flexes.

"This one's done [your real number] shifts on [the run / the truck]. Still bends."

Bend the never-worn one. It cracks. Leave two seconds of silence.

"Never worn. Sat in the shed [how long]."

"It's called hydrolysis. The foam in the sole breaks down whether you wear it or not."

"So that spare pair you're saving for when these die? Go give it a bend."

Put them down.

Let the crack do the work. Nothing about the FAR sole, that one waits on paperwork.

2. Bad advice

First shot

You walking AT the camera, already talking.

Text on screen
YOU'VE PROBABLY GIVEN THIS ADVICE
The script

"The worst advice on the job gets handed out every single day. You've probably said it."

"'They'll wear in.'" (beat) "[What it cost you. One line.]"

"'Harden up.'" (beat) "[Your real one.]"

"'It's just the job.'" (beat) "[Your real one, or a mate's.]"

"'She'll be right.'" (beat) "[Your real one.]"

Cut to a locked-off shot, close.

"It's not the bloke. It's the boot."

"Next time you hear it, look down."

Deadpan. No lecture, you're one of them. Nothing medical you can't back with your own body.

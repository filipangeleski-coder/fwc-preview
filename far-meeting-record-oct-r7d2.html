The offer

  • 50 free pairs, mostly creators: 30 creators, 20 foremen and owners. No payment, no content asked. We ask for honest feedback. If they post, they post. Names go on the Seeding tab.
  • The first 100 at $219 pay in full. Early November each of us messages people we know with a link to a private page. No deposit. When 100 are sold, it closes.
  • We'll all send the same message (Con's idea). It gets written before November.
  • $249 after the first 100. Fil went with $249 so only the first 100 feel special.
  • Referral offer killed. Xavier, Oliver and the others who earned something still get it when the boots land.
  • Warranty 6 months on faults: stitching, laces, the sole coming away. Not normal wear. It matches the factory. Lionel's catch: the factory's 6 months starts when the boots land here, so boots sitting on our shelf use it up.
  • 30 days money-back stays. Wear them to work for 30 days from the day they turn up.

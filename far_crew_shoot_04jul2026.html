The one rule for this batch

Broad but specific. The winners are one exact moment anyone with a job feels, not a moment only a tradie gets. The 5am alarm is the template: a tradie hates it, but so does a nurse, a bus driver, an office worker. Trade skin stays (site, ute, hi-vis, smoko), but the feeling has to be universal. That's why the alarm clip did 78K and the feet-spec stuff died.

How to use

These are options for the content session. Read them, pick what to film, bank what we shoot, bin what we don't, hold the good ones we don't get to for next time. Each bit leads with The Story so you can work out the blocking yourselves.

Driveway, garage, yard only. No filming on any live or active site.

Tentpole · Founder Reveal

The Barrage

Supersedes the 21 Jun dialogue-relay version. HIGH VIS · subtitles ON · no CTA · roles float

The story

Every earlier version of this reveal tried to sell "we all suffered the same betrayal" through dialogue, three blokes taking turns saying a line. That's the boring version, because it's three people asserting something instead of proving it. The barrage proves it: an actual pile of ~10 dead boots is physical, undeniable evidence, nobody has to claim anything a stranger might not believe. The hook is 100% action, dialogue only enters after the visual has landed, and a specific count beats a vague "every boot we've ever owned."

Production note

Doesn't need 10 continuous live throws in one take. Film individual throws from a few angles/distances and quick-cut them, buries the seams and makes the barrage feel bigger and faster than it was. Boots thrown = your own genuinely dead ones, never a FAR boot. FAR boot stays clean, held up at the end as the contrast.

OPTION 1The Count— primary rec
Cold open · shot 1 · no dialogue

Wide, one of you standing dead centre in an empty street, arms crossed, waiting. Beat. A boot flies in from off-screen, thuds down. No reaction. Second. Third. Then relentless — Grant and Lionel either side of frame, hurling dead boots rapid-fire, cut faster each time, until he's standing in a pile of 10+ and hasn't moved.

As the barrage lands, he counts, deadpan, over the chaos

"...six... seven... that's just this year."

Beats

  1. Stops. Silence. He looks down at the pile.
  2. Grant and Lionel step in from either side, empty-handed. Grant: "Every one died the same way." Lionel: "So we stopped buying them."
  3. He kicks the pile. Fil: "And built our own."
  4. Wide all three, boots piled at their feet, one clean FAR boot standing out from the wreckage.
  5. Closer Lionel picks the FAR boot up out of the pile: "Tested on us first. On real sites. Not a lab."
ButtonTosses the FAR boot to camera, hard cut to black on the catch.
OPTION 2Silent

Use if: the barrage footage is strong enough to hold attention with nothing said, or the counting reads try-hard on playback.

Same barrage staging

Zero dialogue during the throw sequence, just the thuds, the growing pile, his unmoved face. No count-callout.

On-screen text, timed to the peakevery boot we've ever owned

Then

  1. Stops. Silence. Beat on the pile.
  2. Cut straight to the wide (Grant + Lionel stepping in), same closing beats as Option 1: "Every one died the same way" / "So we stopped buying them" / "And built our own" / closer / button.
OPTION 3Aftermath First

Use if: you want the strongest possible scroll-stop. Opening on an unexplained image (a bloke silently buried in boots) is a harder curiosity hook than watching the barrage build in real time. Same footage, just re-ordered, costs nothing extra to film.

Cold open · shot 1

Open on the AFTERMATH, one of you already standing silently buried to the shins in a pile of 10+ dead boots, dead serious, staring at camera. No context yet.

On-screen textwe didn't plan this

Beats

  1. Beat. Hard cut: ONE MINUTE EARLIER
  2. Play the barrage in full, Grant and Lionel either side, hurling boots rapid-fire, building back up to the exact frame you opened on.
  3. Land back on the aftermath shot, same framing as the cold open, now it makes sense. Grant: "Every one died the same way." Lionel: "So we stopped buying them."
  4. Fil kicks the pile: "And built our own." Same wide / closer / button as Option 1.

Casting + loop notes

  • Who stands in the middle: open call on the day. The recognisable anchor from That'll Do probably reads funniest getting pelted; Fil in the middle works thematically for the "kicks the pile / built our own" beat. Either works.
  • Loop option (any of the 3): end on one more boot flying in from off-screen at the standing founder, unexplained, right as it cuts to black. Loops back into the cold open on rewatch. Loops drive re-watch, the #1 signal.
  • Cover options: WE GOT SICK OF IT. / EVERY BOOT WE'VE EVER OWNED. / (Option 3 only) WE DIDN'T PLAN THIS.
  • Caption (draft, any option): "Ten boots. Three blokes. Same reason every time. So we built the one that doesn't do this."

The Hot Potato Job

nobody wants it, watch it get passed
Length
Quick-POV, physical relay, ~20-25s
Cast
Ensemble (4+ if possible)
Location
Yard/garage
Props
One visibly cursed/gross/awkward item (blocked drain snake, a bin to empty, whatever reads instantly as "the worst job")

The story

This is the one with a real physical engine, not a talking bit. A job nobody wants gets handed off person to person the second it appears, each one finding an excuse in under a second and shoving it to the next bloke, rapid relay, until it lands on whoever has the least status. Universal: every job, every office, every friend group has had the exact object physically pushed into their hands while everyone else suddenly has somewhere urgent to be. The whole gag is VISUAL, the object moving hand to hand faster than anyone can protest, not a line of dialogue carrying it. Open with the item already mid-handoff, not someone announcing the job.

Cold open · shot 1

The cursed item is already being thrust into someone's hands, mid-motion, no setup, no one explains what it is.

"nah, not it, here-" (already shoving it to the next bloke before he's finished the sentence)

Beats (fast, almost no dialogue, the relay IS the joke)

  1. Second bloke barely touches it, already turning, shoving it on. "yep, nah, here."
  2. Third bloke sees it coming, actively dodges, it gets passed AROUND him to a fourth.
  3. Speed increases each pass, quick cuts, excuses get flimsier and faster ("meeting" / "phone" / just walks off).
  4. Button It lands in the hands of whoever just arrived on site, mid-"hey what's-". Everyone else is already gone. Silence. He looks down at it.
LoopThe silence cuts back to the cold open.
CoverThe Hot Potato Job.
CaptionEvery crew's got one job nobody wants. Watch how fast it moves.

Sat 3 Oct, cut 9. Fil's note on cut 8: the hook moves too fast and nobody can read the text. Only the first three seconds changed.

What changed

  • One thing to read at a time. YOUR LAST PAIR. sits on its own for 1.4 seconds. Then $1,000 lands on the word "thousand" and holds still for 1.3 seconds. At the cut to Fil it drops behind him and drains away on "drain."
  • The word-by-word captions under the hook are gone. In cut 8 they changed every half second right under the hook line, so there were two lots of text fighting each other.
  • Fil's voice now starts at 1 second, after the hook line has been read, not on top of it.
  • Same shots, slower. The drive-over, the pull-out and the boot springing up are all still there, now spread over 2.7 seconds instead of 1.7. The boot hangs in the air for a second while "bucks... down the" plays.
  • Everything from the invoice onwards is cut 8, untouched. The video is now 18.9 seconds.

Why cut 8 was unreadable

That's 11 words in 2.3 seconds across five cuts. People read about 4 words a second when there's nothing else on screen to look at, and subtitle rules for TV never let any line go under about 0.8 of a second.

The honest part

  • The $1,000 goes from bright white to a softer grey when it drops behind Fil, because his shot has the same top shading as the rest of the video. It reads as going into the scene, but you'll notice it.
  • With the sound off, people get YOUR LAST PAIR. then $1,000 then "drain." They never see "That's a thousand bucks" written out.
  • The hook line itself is unchanged. If it still doesn't land once it can be read, the line is the next thing to change, not the speed.
